"Shadowban" is not an official TikTok term. Creators use it for the situation where **videos still post but are hardly shown on the For You feed**, so views drop to a few dozen or zero. TikTok describes most of these cases as content that is *not eligible for recommendation* or an account that is *restricted*.

The good news: you can check this fairly reliably today, without guessing.

## Common signs

- Views on new videos drop sharply and consistently compared with the channel's usual level, for several days.
- A video sits at 0 views for hours after posting.
- In video analytics, the **For You** traffic source almost disappears and views come mostly from your profile or followers.
- Searching your exact caption or your own unique hashtag from another account does not show the video.

One low video is **not** a shadowban. Only suspect one when several videos in a row drop, and they drop in For You traffic.

## 5 checks

### 1. Open "Account status"

Go to **Profile → Menu (☰) → Settings and privacy → Account → Account status** (the exact labels vary slightly by app version). This page lists violations, warnings and restrictions on the account, with their duration if any. If a restriction shows here, that is your answer.

### 2. Check each video for "Not eligible for For You"

Open the video → **…** or its analytics. A limited video usually shows a *not eligible for the For You feed* notice with the reason and an **appeal** button. Also check **Inbox → System notifications**: TikTok notifies you when it removes or limits content.

### 3. Compare traffic sources

Switch to a Creator or Business account (free) to see **Analytics**. For each video, *Traffic source* splits views between For You, your profile, following and search. If For You used to bring 70–90% of views and now brings a few percent across several videos in a row, recommendations are very likely limited.

### 4. Search from another account

From a different account (or a logged-out browser), search your **username** and a **rare hashtag** only you use. Wait a few hours after posting, as new videos take time to be indexed. Missing from both search and the hashtag page is one more sign.

### 5. Rule out other causes

Views also fall because of weaker content, bad timing, a trend that has passed, or audio muted for copyright. Compare with your last 10–20 videos before you conclude anything.

## Why accounts get limited

- Community Guidelines violations: sensitive or misleading content, disguised ads, aggressive external links.
- Unoriginal content: reuploads, stitched compilations of other people's videos, other platforms' watermarks.
- Spam-like behaviour: mass follow/like/comment, engagement tools, posting very often in a short time.
- "Many accounts, one device" signals: several accounts on one phone, constantly changing IPs, an IP that does not match the account's region.
- Copyrighted sounds on a Business account.

## How to recover

1. **Appeal** wrongly flagged videos in the app; delete videos that really break the rules.
2. **Stop spam-like behaviour**: drop engagement tools, slow down follows and likes.
3. **Keep device and IP stable**: one device or one browser profile per account, with one [static proxy](/en/categories/static-proxy) in the account's country, never switched back and forth.
4. **Come back with original content** on your channel's topic. Many creators pause for a few days before posting again; TikTok publishes no fixed duration, so watch Account status instead of counting days.
5. Time-limited restrictions end on their own. If the account is still limited after that and no violation is listed, send feedback through **Report a problem** in settings.

There is no way to "remove a shadowban instantly" — be careful with any service that promises it.

## When to switch accounts

If an account carries several active violations, moving to another account is often faster than waiting. Accounts created long ago are usually less limited at first than freshly registered ones; see [aged TikTok accounts](/en/categories/aged-tiktok) or the packages in [TikTok](/en/categories/tiktok). Whatever account you use, the rules above still decide most of your views.

## Summary

- Check **Account status** and each video's notices first.
- Use **traffic sources** to tell a shadowban from a weak video.
- Keep device, IP and behaviour stable; appeal wrong flags.
